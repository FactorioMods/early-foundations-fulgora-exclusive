# Early Foundations: Fulgora Exclusive

Unlock and build short-pile foundations on Fulgora without metallurgic science or a visit to Vulcanus. This fork supports Fulgora-first factories, including **Any Planet Start** runs that need more building space before travelling to other planets.

**Factorio 2.1 | Space Age required | Version 1.0.1**

## What changes

Short-pile foundation research no longer consumes metallurgic science. It still unlocks after electromagnetic science and costs **500 research units at 20 seconds each**, using automation, logistic, chemical, space and electromagnetic science packs.

Crafting costs remain **4 steel plates, 8 low-density structures and 10 refined concrete**, with a 20-second crafting time. Foundations retain placement on both shallow oil-ocean variants on Fulgora and the original supported water and wetland tiles. Deep oil oceans and lava remain excluded.

Short-pile foundations are listed before advanced foundations in item menus. Item and tile entries share matching artwork based on the actual foundation surface.

## Installation and existing saves

Disable [Early Foundations](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/early-foundations) before enabling this fork. The two mods are mutually exclusive. Existing item, recipe, technology and tile identifiers are retained when switching.

[Any Planet Start](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/any-planet-start) is optional. This fork also works with normal Space Age progression; it changes foundation research rather than the selected starting planet.
